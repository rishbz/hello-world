# SnapMonk 🚀

AI-Powered App Store Screenshot & ASO Generator

SnapMonk helps app developers ship high-converting App Store and Google Play listings without needing a designer.

🌐 Official Website: https://snapmonk.com

---

## ✨ What is SnapMonk?

SnapMonk is an AI-first platform for generating professional app store screenshots, creatives, and ASO assets in minutes.

The workflow is simple:

1. Upload your raw app screenshots
2. Choose a visual direction
3. Let AI generate the complete listing assets

SnapMonk automatically creates:

* marketing copy
* layouts
* device frames
* branded visuals
* localized variants
* store-ready exports

All optimized for:

* Apple App Store
* Google Play Store

## 🔍 Built-in ASO Keyword Research

SnapMonk also includes an AI-powered ASO keyword research tool.

Simply paste an App Store URL and get:

* 50 ranked keyword ideas
* search-focused suggestions
* ASO insights in 30–60 seconds

This helps optimize screenshots and metadata around the keywords users actually search for.
